## Notes

- "Flexible consumption" likely means unused conversions roll over or can burst
- Pricing is flat per conversion regardless of file size or page count
- No mention of image captioning or AI features — pure format conversion
- Concurrency limits are a key differentiator between tiers
- All plans billed annually; monthly pricing likely ~20% higher
