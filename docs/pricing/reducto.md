# Reducto Pricing (as of 2026-05-01)

Source: https://reducto.ai/pricing

## Plans

### Standard
- Pay-as-you-go for early-stage teams
- **15,000 free credits** included
- **$0.015 per credit** after free tier
- Up to 5 seats in Reducto Studio
- No page limits
- Core APIs: Parse, Extract, Edit, Split
- 30+ file types supported

### Growth (Most Popular)
- Custom pricing (contact sales)
- Everything in Standard, plus:
  - Volume discounts
  - Zero data retention agreement option
  - BAA available
  - Premium rate limits and priority requests
  - Priority support (Slack + email)
  - EU/AU data residency endpoints
  - Unlimited Studio seats
  - Studio evaluations

### Enterprise
- Custom pricing and terms
- Everything in Growth, plus:
  - VPC and on-premises deployment
  - Custom MSA and SLA
  - Custom rate limits and throughput
  - Custom processing pipelines
  - Dedicated on-call support
  - RBAC, SSO, SAML

## Notes

- 1 credit = 1 page (assumed, not explicitly stated on pricing page)
- Available via AWS Marketplace
- At $0.015/credit, effective rate is ~$0.015/page — higher than previously estimated
